Vancouver, Canada · sepidhosseini1908@gmail.com

I grew up by the sea in Anzali, a small city in Iran where it rains a lot. I moved to Korea right after high school, stayed long enough for rice, kimchi and a fried egg to remain my favourite comfort food, and eventually moved to Vancouver—apparently I have a thing for rainy cities.

I now have several places I can call home and, somehow, no home at all.

I own an unreasonable number of plants 🪴, take unnecessarily long walks, and have approximately 4,000 photos of light hitting buildings, trees, windows, and other things nobody asked me to photograph.

All of this runs on coffee — an amount best described as “structural.”

During the day, I spend my time convincing very large models to make slightly fewer bad decisions. The models occasionally listen to me. The plants mostly don’t.

☔ current forecast: rain, obviously.

About

I am a Senior Machine Learning Researcher at RBC Borealis. I received my Ph.D. from Simon Fraser University (SFU), where I was advised by Prof. Yasutaka Furukawa. Prior to SFU, I completed my M.Sc. at Seoul National University (SNU) under the supervision of Prof. Cho Nam Ik, and my B.Sc. in Electrical and Computer Engineering at SNU.

My research interests include computer vision, geometric deep learning, anomaly detection, and time series forecasting.
